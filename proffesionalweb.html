How a session works

A simple step-by-step approach focused on understanding.

  1. Send your topic or problem set

    A photo, a PDF, or just the chapter name — whatever you have.

  2. Online / Offline session

    Work through the topic step by step using examples and explanations.

  3. You solve, I check

    You work through similar problems and I correct the reasoning as you go.

  4. You get a short recap

    Notes and practice problems to try before the next session.
